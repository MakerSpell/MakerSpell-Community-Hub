# 🛒 Shop Hub & E-Commerce Production Orders

Designed for print-on-demand sellers and 3D printing service providers managing orders from Trendyol, Shopify, or custom online stores.

---

## 📌 1. Centralized Order Pipeline

- **Automatic Store Sync:** Pulls active customer orders and required product variants straight into your dashboard.
- **Production Status:** Track progress across clear stages:
  - ⏳ *Pending* -> 🖨️ *Printing* -> ✅ *Quality Check* -> 📦 *Shipped*.
- **Specifications:** View material preferences, quantity, customer notes, and due dates at a glance.

---

## 📌 2. One-Click Production Dispatch

When a product G-code is already saved:
- Click **"Dispatch to Printer"** on the order card to route the job to any idle machine in your print farm.
- When finished, MakerSpell automatically updates the order status and sends a completion notification.
