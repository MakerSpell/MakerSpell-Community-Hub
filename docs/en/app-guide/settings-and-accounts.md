# ⚙️ App Preferences, Alerts & Account Management

Manage system performance, notifications, language settings, and account details in MakerSpell settings.

---

## 📌 1. Network & Telemetry Settings

- **Polling Frequency:** Set telemetry refresh rates (optimized for low-power laptops or battery operation).
- **Camera Stream Quality:** Adjust framerate and resolution based on Wi-Fi bandwidth.
- **Language & Localization:** Switch seamlessly between English, Turkish, and 20 other supported global languages.

---

## 📌 2. Audio & Desktop Notifications

- **Print Finished Alerts:** Receive audio chimes and native desktop notifications when a print finishes.
- **Runout / Thermal Alarms:** Urgent sound warnings if filament runs out or nozzle temperatures fluctuate abnormally.
- **Themes:** Toggle between high-contrast Dark Mode and crisp Light Mode.
