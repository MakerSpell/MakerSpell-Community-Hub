# 📂 Model Library & Download Manager

The **Library** screen is your personal 3D digital repository, organizing files collected across 26 platforms or imported from local storage.

---

## 📌 1. Library Overview & Tagging

- **Model Cards:** Interactive cards showcasing high-resolution 3D renders, file formats (STL/3MF/OBJ), file sizes, and author attribution.
- **Custom Collections:** Group models into folders like *"Home Organizers"*, *"Robotics"*, or *"Cosplay"*.
- **Instant Search:** Filter hundreds of models in real-time by keyword, license type, or format.

---

## 📌 2. Integrated Download Manager

When sending models from [makerspell.com](https://www.makerspell.com) to your app:
- The download manager streams multi-part archives silently in the background.
- Compressed `.zip` packages are unpacked on the fly.
- Completed files immediately open in the 3D viewer, ready for slicing.
