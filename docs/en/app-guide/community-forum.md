# Community Forum & Technical Support

MakerSpell includes a dedicated community forum integrated directly into both the mobile application and the web platform. Through the forum, creators, designers, and 3D printing enthusiasts can connect, share tested print profiles, ask troubleshooting questions, and submit feature requests.

---

## Accessing the Forum from the Mobile App

1. Open the **MakerSync** application on your device.
2. Tap the menu icon in the search bar header to open the side navigation drawer.
3. Select **Community Forum**.
4. The forum opens in an optimized, distraction-free view with quick refresh and browser handover options.

---

## Forum Categories & Topics

- **Feature Requests & Ideas:** Submit suggestions for new slicer integrations, printer hardware support, or search filters. Community members can upvote features to help guide the development roadmap.
- **Bug Reports & Troubleshooting:** Report app glitches, unexpected connection drops, or slicer bridge issues using our structured reporting template.
- **3D Printers & Hardware:** Discussions and tips for Creality K1, Snapmaker, Bambu Lab, Voron, and custom DIY printers.
- **Slicers & Print Profiles:** Share and download tested OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio, and Cura profiles.
- **Filament & Print Quality:** Recommendations for PETG, PLA, TPU, ABS, and technical materials.
- **Contests & Community Events:** Participate in official 3D design challenges and earn exclusive profile badges.

---

## Single Sign-On (SSO) Convenience

Your MakerSpell ID works across the main platform, mobile app, and community forum. You do not need to register a separate account or remember multiple passwords.
